D2 Classification Key

VERIFIED Confirmed at the named primary source as of the date shown.

PLAUSIBLE Consistent across named sources; specifics (eligibility, dollar limits, fund availability) change annually. Confirm at the named source before relying.

STATE Program framework is federal; numbers and operational details vary by state. Call your county, state agency, or state SHIP counselor.

SPECIFICATION Pathway or pattern guidance, not a verified fact about a specific program.

REVIEW DUE The scheduled review date for this figure has passed, or a scheduled change has occurred since it was verified. The figure is shown as originally verified. Confirm at the primary source before relying on it.

A program may be VERIFIED in its framework while a specific income threshold within it is PLAUSIBLE. Dated figures show an “as of” date; the app flags them automatically when their review date passes.
