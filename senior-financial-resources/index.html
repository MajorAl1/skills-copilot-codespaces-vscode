D2 Classification Key

VERIFIED Confirmed at the named primary source as of the compile date.

PLAUSIBLE Consistent across named sources; specifics (eligibility, dollar limits, fund availability) change annually. Confirm at the named source before relying.

STATE Program framework is federal; numbers and operational details vary by state. Call your county, state agency, or state SHIP counselor.

A program may be VERIFIED in its framework while a specific income threshold within it is PLAUSIBLE.
