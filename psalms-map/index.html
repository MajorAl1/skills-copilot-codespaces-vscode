The Psalms a map for the class

One hundred and fifty poems, in five books.

The five books are not a study aid somebody added later. Four of them end with a short burst of praise that belongs to no psalm's argument — a doxology, stitched on. Find the seam and you have found the division. Tap any psalm below.
